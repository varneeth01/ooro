# OORO search intent map — 11 Nov 2026

## Evidence and limits

This map is an editorial prioritization based on live organic-result inspection, the existing OORO URL set, product fit, and public competitor pages. No authorized Search Console, Keyword Planner, analytics, or paid keyword database is connected. Search volume, CPC, competition, positions and traffic are therefore not estimated here. Recheck query demand and landing-page performance in Search Console after verification.

Broad “digital advertising agency Bangalore” results commonly mean SEO, paid search and social agency services. OORO does not offer those services; the query is intentionally not assigned a target page. Auto-rickshaw result pages commonly sell exterior wraps. OORO's distinct product is a passenger-facing screen inside an auto or cab, subject to inventory review. Pages make that distinction explicit.

## Intent and SERP notes

- Broad “advertising in Bangalore” is mixed between agencies, listings and media vendors. OORO should use a clear city page and describe its actual product near the top rather than imply a full-service agency.
- “Digital advertising agency Bangalore” has substantial intent mismatch with OORO. No dedicated page or exact-match landing page is planned.
- “Auto-rickshaw advertising Bangalore” currently surfaces print/exterior vendor content as well as media buying. Explain interior vs exterior placements and answer availability and evidence questions.
- Price queries need transparent factors rather than fabricated rate tables. Only the public ₹500 input floor is verifiable in the existing self-serve flow, and it is not a media quote.
- Industry queries remain examples inside a useful shared small-business page until distinct product eligibility and first-party evidence support genuinely different pages.

## Review cadence

After Search Console verification: monthly inspect query impressions/clicks, page-index status, brand vs non-brand, Bangalore vs Bengaluru phrasing, `/advertise` organic sessions and qualified enquiries. Consolidate rather than create pages if queries cannibalize.
