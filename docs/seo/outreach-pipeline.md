## Workflow / measurement

1. Confirm the facts, spokesperson consent and the claim boundaries with OORO operations.
2. Review the outlet's current editorial policy and use its stated submission route.
3. Record date, contact route, angle, target page, response and whether any placement is editorial or sponsored. Do not send until operator approval.
4. Never request keyword-rich anchor text or a dofollow link. Accept editorial discretion; qualify sponsored links.
5. Measure relevant referring domains and qualified referral visits/enquiries in authorized analytics; do not use raw link totals as the objective.
