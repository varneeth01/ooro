## Live crawl snapshot

The public sitemap exposed 11 URLs before this branch's changes. A crawler saw successful responses for those URLs plus login/signup URLs; 0 URL failures in that crawl. Several existing pages inherited root-level metadata. Admin/API/dashboard paths are disallowed; account and operational paths need noindex as well as access control when crawlers can reach them. Robots is not an access-control system.

## Technical implementation report

- Canonicals use `https://theooro.com` as `metadataBase` and per-page paths.
- Sitemap lists public, indexable commercial pages and published guides; privacy/terms placeholder copy is excluded.
- Auth/verification, private status, payment completion and placeholder legal pages carry noindex metadata. Private app/admin layouts already opt out. Robots disallows authenticated app/admin/API paths.
- Organization and WebSite JSON-LD are factual and global. Article JSON-LD is added only on guide pages; no FAQPage, LocalBusiness, fake reviews, team social `sameAs` or VideoObject without verifiable publication facts.
- Breadcrumbs are visible navigation; no redundant schema graph is added.
- Original photos/video are used. Video uses native controls, `playsInline`, `preload="none"`, and a poster. Exact video caption needs depend on its audio track and should be reviewed with the media owner.
- No account analytics/GSC credentials found in connected tools. Search Console setup: verify `theooro.com` Domain property via DNS; submit `https://theooro.com/sitemap.xml`; inspect canonical/indexing for public landing pages and auth noindex handling; review Page Indexing and Core Web Vitals after enough field traffic. If analytics is added, confirm current privacy notice/consent requirements and avoid duplicate tags.
- Local SEO: no eligible staffed business address or authorized Google Business Profile was verified. Do not create a virtual-office or fake-location listing. Operator should first verify business eligibility/location and GBP ownership, then align actual name/category/contact/website and genuine photos.

## LCP test conditions and result

Three before and three after runs were executed against local production (`next start`, not dev), Lighthouse CLI 13.6.0 / Chromium 146 headless on a Linux VM with SwiftShader, 390×844 CSS viewport, device scale factor 2, Lighthouse simulated mobile network (150ms RTT, ~1.6Mbps down) and 4× CPU slowdown. Exact traces and reports are in local untracked `artifacts/visual-acceptance/`.

| Run | Simulated LCP before | Simulated LCP after | Performance score before/after | Trace candidate after |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3.475s | 3.158s | .85 / .93 | 231ms |
| 2 | 3.460s | 2.859s | .87 / .96 | 214ms |
| 3 | 3.312s | 3.084s | .89 / .93 | 224ms |
| Median | **3.460s** | **3.084s** | **.87 / .93** | **224ms** |

Identified LCP on each pass: original hero image `ooro-bengaluru-auto-01.webp`, emitted as WebP with `fetchpriority=high`; optimized mobile response approximately 63.6KB transferred. `sizes` now reflects mobile content width (`calc(100vw - 40px)`) rather than full viewport. Quality is unchanged.

Median Lighthouse LCP subparts before/after (timings do not sum to Lighthouse's simulated LCP metric):

| Subpart | Before | After | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| TTFB | 6.9ms warm median (one cold 164.8ms) | 6.4ms | Local server warm and fast; not production VPS TTFB. |
| Resource load delay | 14.5ms | 34.8ms | Small absolute time in trace, but higher than baseline. |
| Resource load duration | 16.7ms | 13.1ms | 63.6KB optimized hero; not dominant. |
| Element render delay | 436.3ms | 165.2ms | Largest measured subpart; down ~62%, though Lighthouse modeled LCP still misses target. |

Observed trace LCP candidates before were 775/475/466ms (median 475ms), after 231/214/224ms (median 224ms). These are trace candidate event timings, distinct from Lighthouse's simulated metric; they are not production field metrics. Lighthouse CLS after was 0 on all three runs, TBT 96/25/95ms. Laboratory measurements do not establish production p75 Core Web Vitals. Mobile LCP target remains unmet by Lighthouse simulated LCP.
