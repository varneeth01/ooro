## Source standards

- Cite primary rules or standards when making a specific rule claim. The outdoor guide links to [BBMP's official Advertisement Bye-laws](https://site.bbmp.gov.in/PDF/whatsnew/BBMPAdvertisementBye_laws_English.pdf) and [ASCI's Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code/).
- The BBMP document includes vehicle advertising provisions; it is not interpreted here as automatically applying to OORO interior screens. Confirm the specific medium and responsible authority.
- OORO methodology numbers are OORO planning assumptions, not independent research. Any future reuse needs visible attribution and limitations.
- No testimonials, case-study outcomes, audience totals, guaranteed availability, ROI, or fabricated neighborhood coverage.
- The OORO field journal uses original `public/field` photos/video and explicitly says these are installation documentation, not campaign delivery evidence.
